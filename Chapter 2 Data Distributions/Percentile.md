In statistics, a k-th percentile, is a score (e.g., a data point) below which a given percentage k of all scores in its frequency distribution exists ("exclusive" definition).

一个*k*-th percentile的数据，是指在一个分布中有*k*%的数据低于这个数。比如最大的数据是100th percentile，而最小的数据是0th percentile.
